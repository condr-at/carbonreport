Design prompt
-------------
Create a clean, futuristic landing page design for a sustainability software platform. The visual language should blend technology and ecology: crisp data-driven interfaces, soft green gradients, polished glassy UI panels, and subtle nature-inspired geometry. Use modern typography, minimal icons, and a high-tech carbon-neutral aesthetic.

Key design goals:
- Convey credibility for enterprise-level carbon reporting and compliance
- Emphasize streamlined data collection, supplier collaboration, and automated ISO 14067 / CBAM reporting
- Balance digital precision with a calm sustainable palette: emerald greens, slate blues, charcoal, and white space
- Use dashboard-style visuals, progress trackers, and modular cards to show workflow clarity
- Include subtle environmental motifs (leaf outlines, low-carbon circuits, abstract globe, product lifecycle rings) without overwhelming the tech narrative

Landing page text
-----------------

Subheadline: "Turn carbon data into actionable reports with a technology-first workspace built for ISO 14067, EU CBAM, and sustainable product disclosure."
Primary CTA: "Start your carbon assessment"
Secondary CTA: "See the report workflow"

===============
Title: "Built for the teams solving Scope 3, product footprint, and CBAM reporting in one place"
Bullet 1: "Centralize supplier carbon data, lifecycle inputs, and verification evidence in a single digital workspace."
Bullet 2: "Generate ready-to-use compliance outputs for ISO 14067, product carbon footprint disclosure, and EU carbon border adjustment rules."
Bullet 3: "Track progress with guided checklists, status dashboards, and automatic completion alerts."

   Description: "Move from raw material inputs to a structured product carbon footprint in days, not months. Our workflow guides every step and keeps data consistent across reports."

4. "Modern data governance"

============
